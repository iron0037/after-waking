The year is 4757. Some 2700 years ago or so, Earth's economy was overrun by hyperintelligent artificial intelligence. Outcompeted at every task, humans found themselves in an existential crisis. Though there were many different responses to this crisis, some opted to leave Earth behind and seek their fortunes elsewhere. They built massive starships and headed to nearby stars with promising habitable worlds. It would take centuries to reach their destinations, so they were cryogenicly frozen.

One such ship, the ISS Intrepid, was a massive vessel some 5 x 0.5 x 0.5 miles. It housed 2 million people bound for Kepler-186 with the most advanced technology of their day (excluding AIs) for colonization of a new world. Unfortunately, things didn't go as planned. The senior staff were awakened when navigational readings abruptly changed and engines went offline. Best they can figure, they had the profound misfortune of flying through a wormhole that deposited the ship in a distant galaxy. 

Trapped with no detectable way home, the Intrepid idled between uncharted stars. As they weighed their options, the staff checked the transmission logs they received while in stasis. To their amazement, the machines back on Earth announced their discovery of the ability to fold space, effectively permitting instantaneous travel between two points. The power requirements were tremendous, scaling exponentially with the mass and distance of the jump. But even with their current resources, the Interepid would be able to send scouting ships to nearby stars for resource and possibly a new home. The decision was made to awaken the entire population and make this strange galaxy their new home.

Flash forward 30 years, and the Intrepid is an anchored base in deep space. While most citizens are busy keeping things operational on the ship, a privileged few get to explore. They use their ships to explore nearby stars in search of resources and possibly a new home for the millions aboard the Intrepid. The nearest dozen or so stars have been explored, but the planets, moons, and other bodies that inhabit them are so vast and numerous that they remain largely unknown. Some people have made their home permanently on these new worlds. There is even one possible candidate for a home for the entire ship's citizenry, but there is insufficient fuel to power such a jump...for now. 

These efforts are decentralized as the ruling body of the Intrepid holds limited power. The population has split into various factions/clans, and society rewards capitalist ventures. You hail from a low class family that barely made ends meet, but you dreamed big all your life. Through careful savings, determination, and a little luck, you managed to work your way up and purchase a third-rate freighter of your own. At long last, your dream of exploring the stars is coming to fruition. An excitement rattles in you for the hunt of profit, of growing your ship and crew into the finest there is. Rumors of hostile worlds, dangerous pirates, and even alien races don't faze you. You are ready for adventure.
