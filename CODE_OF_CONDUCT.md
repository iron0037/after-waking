# Code of conduct

After Waking is a small hobby project, and everyone who plays it, reports a bug or sends a change should feel welcome here.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the game, to GitHub or to programming.
- Criticize the work, not the person. "This puzzle is unfair" is useful feedback; insults aren't.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Don't share anyone's personal information without their permission.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Slurs, or demeaning jokes about anyone's identity, background or body.
- Sexual content or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.

## Where this applies

In the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and comments, and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting a problem

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or report the account to GitHub directly. The maintainer reviews every report. Reports are handled privately, and nobody is punished for reporting in good faith.

## What happens next

Depending on how serious it is, the maintainer may edit or remove the content, give a warning, lock the thread, or block the account from the project.

This code of conduct takes its spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
