Main Game Loop
* Travel to star systems
* Explore planets, moons, asteroids, etc
* Extract resources, artifacts, and specimins
* Return to the Interepid and sell resources
* Upgrade ship and crew

Secondary Game Loops
* Go on posted jobs (deliver cargo, shuttle passengers, scout uncharted worlds, attack something/someone)
* Fight pirates and other freighters in space battles, earning a reputation unique amongst each clan
* Establish outposts loyal to you that grow in strength and prosperity

Paths to Victory
* Locate the wormhole that brought the Intrepid to this galaxy and gather enough resources to have it fly back through
* Find a suitable world for habitation, provide enough resources to the Intrepid to have it jump to the destination
* Build an outpost of your own on a distant world and have it thrive
* Find an advanced alien civilization and serve as an intermediary for negotiations for them to bestow a new home to the humans
* Become the head of your clan on the Intrepid
* A Matrix-like twist as you uncover clues revealing that you're all in a simulation built by AI
